# 1.2 Domain glossary

**Goal:** learn the vocabulary of the code, defined by the code.
**Time:** about 10 minutes.

Paste:

```
Use the code-explorer agent and the build-glossary skill. Build a glossary of the core domain concepts of this shop: 15 terms at most. Define each one from the code, not from general e-commerce knowledge. Mark any term that is confusing or easy to mix up with another one.
```

**Then, on your own:** choose two terms you did not know. Open the class the glossary cites and check the definition against its fields.
